### 1.1 — Monitoring Changes in Mixed Venous Oxygen Saturation Using Photoplethysmography on the Sternum — A Proof of Concept Study *(previously surfaced — first logged 2026-09-08)*
- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived — not page-confirmed (PubMed egress-blocked). Likely the Scandinavian intraosseous-sternal-PPG group behind the earlier porcine sternal-oximetry work (*J. Clin. Monit. Comput.* 10.1007/s10877-023-00980-z).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · venue **not page-confirmed** (⚠ search-derived). PubMed 42527850.
- **Link / DOI:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42527850/ ⚠ egress-blocked; DOI not captured.
- **Relevance:** **8/10** — non-invasive, continuous **true mixed-venous SvO₂** with a pulmonary-artery-catheter reference; a genuinely new anatomical site (sternum) for venous oximetry.
- **Novelty:** Exploits **sternal intramedullary (intraosseous) blood**, whose saturation tracks central/mixed-venous saturation, read **transcutaneously with a PPG probe** on the sternum. ### 2.2 — Arterial Blood-Mediated Deep-Tissue Photoacoustic Oximetry (APM+): In-Vivo Human Radial-Vein sO₂ *(previously surfaced)*
- **Authors:** Sastry, Olick-Gibson et al.

### 2.3 — Oxygenation Heterogeneity Facilitates Spatiotemporal Flow-Pattern Visualization Inside Human Blood Vessels Using PACT *(previously surfaced)*

### 2.4 — Adaptive Spectroscopic Visible-Light OCT for Clinical Retinal Oximetry (ADS-vis-OCT) *(previously surfaced)*
- **Year / Venue:** 2023 · *Communications Medicine* (Nature), s43856-023-00288-8; PubMed 37095177; PMC10126115. ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata via snippet.
- **Link:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10126115/
- **Relevance:** **8/10** — resolves *per-vessel arterial and venous* retinal sO₂ non-invasively.
- **Novelty:** Adaptively removes vessel-specific spectral contaminants; RMSE ≈ 2.1 % vs pulse oximeter in major arteries, repeatability SD ≈ 2.3 % in *veins*; ex-vivo phantom agreement within 1 % bias across 0–100 % sO₂. Demonstrated across glaucoma, ischemia, DR, CRVO, sickle-cell retinopathy.
- **Integration insights:** Sets the accuracy ceiling for optical venous oximetry in an accessible vascular bed; the adaptive spectral-correction idea is transferable to PA/DOT venous inversion where spectral coloring dominates error (cf. §2.2).

### 2.5 — Rapid Quantitative Imaging of Heterogeneous Tissue Hemoglobin Dynamics Using Spatial Frequency Domain Imaging *(⭐ NEW to corpus this scan — first logged 2026-09-11 · SFDI, the named technique; venous-extraction-relevant but tissue-level)*
- **Authors:** Gordon T. Kennedy, Rebecca Rowland, Amaan Mazhar, David J. Cuccia, Anthony J. Durkin (Beckman Laser Institute, UC Irvine / Modulated Imaging lineage — the canonical SFDI-inventor group). ⚠ author list snippet-confirmed on Karger + PMC index; not page-verified (egress-blocked).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***Journal of Vascular Research*** 63(4):255–264 (published 2026-07-16); **PMC13245941**; **PubMed 41824643**; Karger. ⚠ pages egress-blocked; metadata cross-checked on ≥3 snippets (Karger + PMC + PubMed).
- **Link / DOI:** https://karger.com/jvr/article/63/4/255/946265/ · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13245941/ · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41824643/
- **Relevance:** **6/10** — **tissue-level StO₂, not intravascular SvO₂** (flagged), but the vascular-occlusion-test design directly interrogates the **venous / oxygen-extraction** compartment, and SFDI is one of the task's explicitly-named target techniques with no recent (2026) representative yet in the ranked five; included on that basis.
- **Novelty:** Wide-field **SFDI** maps absolute StO₂ and total hemoglobin — resolved into a **superficial papillary-dermis compartment (HbT1)** and a **deeper compartment (HbT2)** — through a forearm **vascular occlusion test (VOT)** in 13 subjects (baseline → 4 min occlusion → release). Key result: **StO₂ differs significantly between microvascular-only and macrovascular ROIs at all time points**, with **microvascular ROIs showing the larger StO₂ drop during occlusion (higher oxygen extraction)**. The paper's thesis is that **spatial heterogeneity must be accounted for** when comparing tissue hemodynamics across time and subjects — a single ROI misrepresents the extraction signal.
- **Integration insights:** Three concrete contributions to the venous-oximetry program. (1) **The VOT is a venous-compartment assay**: during occlusion the StO₂ decline rate is set by tissue O₂ consumption draining the venous-weighted microvasculature, so SFDI's microvascular ROI is effectively reading a spatially-resolved *venous* desaturation — the imaging analogue of the respiration-isolated venous signal in §1.2/§1.3. (2) **Macro- vs microvascular ROI separation is the spatial version of the artery/vein disambiguation problem** that PACT (§2.3) and vis-OCT (§2.4) solve at the single-vessel scale — SFDI brings a fast, wide-field, contact-free route to the same distinction over a large tissue field. (3) **The HbT1/HbT2 depth split** parallels the depth-compensation ambition of the PA community (ISDC, standing leads) — evidence that venous-weighted signals live preferentially in the deeper compartment, which is exactly where transcutaneous NIRS (§1.2) and deep PA (§2.2) must reach. Caveat to carry forward: this is **volume-averaged tissue StO₂**, so it informs *how to read* the venous extraction signal, not an absolute SvO₂ number.

> **Standing lead — carried from 09-10 (PA-microscopy instrumentation, venous-transferable):** *Improved Sensitivity in Large Field-of-View Multispectral Laser-Scanning Photoacoustic Microscopy for Measuring Oxygen Saturation In Vivo* (Zafar, Khansari, Manwar, Avanaki; ***J. Biophotonics*** 19(3):e202500378, 2026; 10.1002/jbio.202500378; PMC12980567; PubMed 41213654). Splits sO₂ into an **isosbestic pair (532/545 nm)** and a **deoxyHb-dominant pair (545/558 nm)**, buying SNR precisely in the low-sO₂/venous regime — sensitivity bought *spectrally, not thermally* (complements the fluence-ceiling result below).
> **Standing lead — PA metrology / ground-truth:** *Blood-mimicking dye phantoms for evaluating photoacoustic oximetry accuracy* (Zhou, Wang, Wear, Pfefer, Jokerst, Vogt; ***Photoacoustics*** 2026, online 2026-01-08; PMC12854982; PubMed 41624274; PII S2213597926000017). NIR746A + IRA980 dye mixtures reproduce whole-blood PA oximetry error (RMSD 4–18 % across sO₂) — a stable, non-clotting ground-truth for deep-vein PA methods.
> **Standing lead — NIRS metrology / ground-truth:** *Inline dissolved-gas-derived oxygen saturation reference for dynamic blood phantoms at adult-head optical properties (IPCR)* (Univ. Zurich BORL; ***Biomed. Opt. Express*** 2026, online 2026-08-01; PMC13481067; PubMed 42609951). Continuous physicochemical SO₂ reference (median uncertainty **2.1 %**; RMSE 3.5–4.2 % vs FD-NIRS) that tracks *dynamic* desaturation into the venous range.
> **Standing lead — carried:** *Maximum Fluence for Accurate Functional PAM* (10.1016/j.pacs.2026.100851; PMC13319516) — the venous-compartment-limited fluence ceiling; and *Frequency-domain PAM with resonant transducer + interferometric modulation* (10.1016/j.pacs.2026.100834; PMC13156710) — <2 % within-group sO₂ variation into the deoxygenated range.

Each modality attacks it at a different scale: vis-OCT (§2.4) and PACT (§2.3) resolve individual vessels; respiration-isolated NIRS (§1.2) and the IJV-pulse device (§1.3) isolate the venous pulsation in time; APM+ (§2.2) uses the artery as a fluence anchor. Today's SFDI paper adds the **spatial-heterogeneity axis**: a vascular occlusion test read with wide-field SFDI shows that **microvascular (venous-weighted) and macrovascular ROIs desaturate differently**, and that averaging them away destroys the extraction signal. That is the same lesson the vessel-resolving methods learned, now stated at tissue scale — and it explains *why* the surface-NIRS methods in Topic 1 are trend-grade rather than absolute: they integrate over exactly the heterogeneity SFDI here makes visible.

**The three-layer error budget, restated.** The corpus now cleanly separates venous-oximetry error into (1) an **input/fluence bound** (maximum-fluence PAM ceiling; APM+ fluence referencing; ISDC depth compensation), (2) a **readout/sensitivity bound** in the low-sO₂ regime (deoxyHb-dominant LS-PAM wavelength pairing; FD-PAM resonant transducer), and (3) a **spatial/compartment bound** — to which today's SFDI VOT paper is the newest contributor.
