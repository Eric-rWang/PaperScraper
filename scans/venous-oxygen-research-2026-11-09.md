### 1.1 — Non-invasive, Continuous Venous Oxygen Saturation and Oxygen Extraction Estimation from the Internal Jugular Vein *(previously surfaced — the flagship new-generation true-venous device; re-confirmed this run)*
- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived (bioengineering group associated with the jugular-optical-sensing line).
- **Year / Venue:** 2025 · **IEEE EMBC 2025** proceedings. IEEE Xplore doc 11254941; PubMed 41335695; DOI 10.1109/EMBC58623.2025.11254941. ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata from search-index snippets (SvO₂ estimates **65.70–80.91 %**, 6 healthy adults, flexible sensor array reading IJV pulsations upright and supine; peripheral veins non-pulsatile so standard pulse oximetry cannot read SvO₂).
- **Link:** https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11254941/ · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41335695/ · https://doi.org/10.1109/embc58623.2025.11254941
- **Relevance:** **8.5/10** — directly targets non-invasive, continuous **true venous** sO₂ at the IJV plus cerebral O₂ extraction.
- **Novelty:** Flexible/conformal neck sensor array reads the **IJV pulse + breathing-induced modulation** (peripheral veins being non-pulsatile) to estimate SvO₂ ≈ **65.7–80.9 %** (SD 1.67 % for IJV-pulse, 4.26 % for breathing modulation) and a per-subject **cerebral O₂-extraction ratio (O₂ER)** in 6 healthy adults; co-registers carotid artery and IJV at one neck site. Authors flag artery–vein **crosstalk mitigation** and larger-cohort blood-gas validation as next steps.
- **Integration insights:** The extraction-aware wearable endpoint of the whole Topic-1 program; the artery serves as an in-situ reference (same logic as APM+, §2.2). Sibling registry trial **NCT06004466** (National Taiwan University Hospital — plethysmographic device combining ultrasound with optical tissue estimates for continuous non-invasive IJV saturation, re-hit this run) remains the independent clinical-translation counterpart; no published results yet. The UT-Austin **wearable optical e-tattoo** (2023 EMBC 10.1109/embc40787.2023.10340010; 2025 patent US2025/0025074) is the conformal-array sibling pursuing the same neck artery–vein pair; the **Vanderbilt 48-channel PPG e-tattoo** line is the scaled continuation. **Standing context:** the NIH **K99EB035645** award (carotid+IJV → OEF/CMRO₂) is the funded continuation of exactly this O₂ER endpoint via the §2.1 PA route — a grant, not a paper.

### 1.2 — Near-Infrared Spectroscopy for Estimating Mixed Venous Oxygen Saturation and Cardiac Output During Off-Pump Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting: A Prospective Observational Study *(previously surfaced — first logged 2026-09-08; re-confirmed this run)*
- **Authors:** Oh et al. ⚠ author line search-derived — not page-confirmed (PubMed/Springer egress-blocked).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***Journal of Clinical Monitoring and Computing*** (Springer); DOI **10.1007/s10877-026-01477-1**. PubMed 42606675. ⚠ metadata via index snippet ("published online August 2026; jugular rSjvO₂ bias 2.0 %, LoA −14.9/+18.9 %; baseline-calibrated cerebral NIRS the more promising surrogate; NIRS cardiac output clinically insufficient").
- **Link / DOI:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10877-026-01477-1 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42606675/
- **Relevance:** **7.5/10** — non-invasive SvO₂ surrogate via **jugular + cerebral NIRS**, validated head-to-head against **pulmonary-artery-catheter SvO₂** in adults.
- **Novelty:** Tests two non-invasive NIRS surrogates in 25 OPCAB patients — (a) **rSjvO₂** over the left IJV (bias 2.0 %, wide LoA −14.9/+18.9 %) and (b) **baseline-calibrated cerebral NIRS (cal-rScO₂)** on the forehead (bias 0.8 %, LoA −7.8/+9.4 %); the calibrated cerebral measure reached **AUC 0.970 for detecting SvO₂ < 65 %** (sensitivity 95.9 %, specificity 89.8 %). **NIRS-derived cardiac output failed** (≈33 % error). Authors suggest ultrasound pre-screening to select patients in whom a sensor over the IJV is anatomically feasible.
- **Integration insights:** The most rigorous 2026 adult validation yet of jugular/cerebral NIRS as an SvO₂ *trend* monitor. Baseline-calibrated cerebral NIRS **out-tracked a sensor placed right over the IJV** — a strong argument for the vessel-*resolving* imaging of Topic 2 over surface averaging. A 2023 retrospective (*Sci. Rep.* s41598-023-49078-1 / PMC10709586) independently reports cerebral rScO₂ tracking SvO₂ changes. Clean SvO₂ < 65 % discrimination is the clinically actionable low-SvO₂-alarm endpoint.

### 1.3 — Monitoring Changes in Mixed Venous Oxygen Saturation Using Photoplethysmography on the Sternum — A Proof of Concept Study *(previously surfaced — first logged 2026-09-08; re-confirmed this run)*
- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived — not page-confirmed. Likely the Scandinavian intraosseous-sternal-PPG group behind the earlier porcine sternal-oximetry work (*J. Clin. Monit. Comput.* 10.1007/s10877-023-00980-z).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing*** (Springer); DOI **10.1007/s11517-026-03648-3**. PubMed 42527850. ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata via Springer index snippet ("calibrated against pulmonary-artery SvO₂ in anesthetized pigs during hypoxia and haemorrhage; further technical development warranted").
- **Link / DOI:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11517-026-03648-3 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42527850/
- **Relevance:** **8/10** — non-invasive, continuous **true mixed-venous SvO₂** with a pulmonary-artery-catheter reference; a genuinely new anatomical site (sternum) for venous oximetry.
- **Novelty:** Exploits **sternal intramedullary (intraosseous) blood**, whose saturation tracks central/mixed-venous saturation, read **transcutaneously with a PPG probe** on the sternum. In anesthetized pigs, SvO₂ was driven down by stepwise hypoxia and staged hemorrhage while the non-invasive sternal signal (nSsO₂) was calibrated against pulmonary-artery SvO₂: **linear nSsO₂–SvO₂ correlation, ~87 % concordance** for tracking SvO₂ changes.
- **Integration insights:** A distinct physical route to non-invasive venous sO₂: the **bone marrow acts as a naturally venous-weighted optical reservoir**, sidestepping artery–vein disambiguation — the surface analogue of the deep mucosal reservoirs used by the esophageal-RRS and PA-TEE probes. The 2025 bone-marrow MSOT paper (Nano Letters, PMC12893716; Topic-2 standing lead) independently demonstrated that bone-marrow sO₂ is non-invasively optoacoustically measurable. The Noninvasix **LIVOx** chest-worn central-venous-oximetry probe (FDA breakthrough designation Feb 2022) and the NIRSense CHF sternal wearable (NCT07874386) are two further independent bets on the chest/sternal site.

### 2.1 — Noninvasive In Vivo Photoacoustic Measurement of Internal Jugular Venous Oxygenation in Humans *(previously surfaced — the Topic-2 anchor; re-confirmed this run)*
- **Authors:** Garcia-Uribe et al. (Wang lab, WUSTL/Caltech). ⚠ arXiv egress-blocked; abstract + author line re-confirmed this run on index snippets.
- **Year / Venue:** 2023 · preprint arXiv:2303.10775 (+ SPIE/Caltech-WUSTL lineage; precursor SPIE 8943E..1M, 2014).
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10775 · https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.10775 · https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/5jp3j-jnz35
- **Relevance:** **9/10** — human, in vivo, PA, *venous* (IJV).
- **Novelty:** Dual-wavelength photoacoustic tomography co-registered with ultrasound (5 fps) in 7 healthy volunteers; average internal-jugular sijvO₂ **72 ± 7 %** (per-volunteer 62–79 %; a conventional PA-amplitude method gives ≈66–67 % IJV vs ≈96 % carotid). >90 % of IJV drainage comes from intracranial structures, so the IJV reading can track global brain oxygen consumption; framed as a non-invasive alternative to fluoroscopy-guided jugular catheterization. Authors note extension to hepatic, femoral and renal veins. A larger PA/US cohort (SKKU; 165 carotids in 117 volunteers) reports IJV **71.3 ± 6.4 %** vs carotid **93.0 ± 3.5 %** (re-confirmed this run via the SKKU multimodal-PAUS listing).
- **Integration insights:** The reference demonstration that deep-vein sO₂ is PA-measurable in humans. Everything else in Topic 2 either improves its quantification (GenMC standing lead; APM+ §2.2), extends the flow/vessel-type readout (PACT §2.3; ULM+PA preprint), moves to another bed (retina §2.4, skin microvasculature §2.5), or translates it to a wearable (LIVOx / e-tattoo, Topic-1 standing context). **Standing context:** the NIH **K99EB035645** award (carotid + IJV PA → OEF/CMRO₂) is the funded continuation of exactly this line.

### 2.2 — Arterial Blood-Mediated Deep-Tissue Photoacoustic Oximetry (APM+): In-Vivo Human Radial-Vein sO₂ *(previously surfaced; re-confirmed this run)*
- **Authors:** Sastry et al. (Wang lab, Caltech). ⚠ metadata from Caltech PDF header + PubMed/Wiley snippet (received Jan 2026, accepted Jun 8 2026; re-confirmed this run via the coilab.caltech.edu PDF listing).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · *Advanced Science* (Wiley), 10.1002/advs.76366; PMC13334587; PubMed 42397001. Online early July 2026.
- **Link:** https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.76366 · https://coilab.caltech.edu/documents/34878/Sastry-2026-AdvancedScience.pdf · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42397001/
- **Relevance:** **8.5/10** — human, in vivo, PA, radial artery–vein pair with *venous* sO₂ reported.
- **Novelty:** Uses the arterial blood of an accompanying artery (high, known saturation) as an in-situ spectral reference to correct wavelength-dependent fluence ("spectral coloring") for the adjacent deep vein. In 8 healthy adults (dual-wavelength 3D PAT of the wrist), APM+ gave a **median venous sO₂ of 72.3 % (IQR 8.9 %)**; the LUM comparator gave 75.2 % with much wider spread (IQR 34.4 %). Phantom (true 75 %): **APM+ 72 ± 2 %**; phantom median error **2.9 % (APM+) vs 9.8 % (LUM)**; validated through up to 1.5 cm ex vivo tissue (figures re-confirmed this run via search snippet). Authors note the single-arterial-prior approach yields one arterial value rather than a full A/V map and is less accurate for darker skin tones; they propose extending to the **carotid–IJV pair plus flow to estimate oxygen consumption** — the same O₂ER endpoint as §1.1 — noting "most major arteries have accompanying veins."
- **Integration insights:** The artery-as-reference trick is the PA analogue of the arterio-venous compliance idea (§1.5) and the co-registered artery of §1.1. Its phantom-error figures remain the best in-corpus demonstration that per-vessel fluence correction beats linear unmixing for the venous compartment. The TU-Delft "known chromophore as fluence marker" line (Thomas et al., *Photoacoustics* 2025) is the independent methodological precursor.

- **Relevance:** **8/10** — human, intravascular sO₂ heterogeneity and flow, PACT; recovers the parabolic laminar-flow wavefront inside a deep vein in vivo.

### 2.4 — Adaptive Spectroscopic Visible-Light OCT for Clinical Retinal Oximetry (ADS-vis-OCT) *(previously surfaced; re-confirmed this run)*
- **Authors:** Rubinoff et al. ⚠ metadata from PMC/Nature snippet.
- **Year / Venue:** 2023 · *Communications Medicine* (Nature), s43856-023-00288-8; PubMed 37095177; PMC10126115.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-023-00288-8 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37095177/
- **Relevance:** **8/10** — resolves *per-vessel arterial and venous* retinal sO₂ non-invasively.
- **Novelty:** Adds an adaptive spectral-contamination-removal step to vis-OCT, giving accurate, repeatable sO₂ in retinal arteries and veins with no pre-/post-calibration in vivo (phantom bias ≈1 % across 0–100 %; RMSE vs pulse oximeter 2.1 % major arteries; repeat SD 2.5 % small arteries / 2.3 % small veins in 18 volunteers). ### 2.5 — Recording Skin Oxygenation by Dual-Wavelength Ultra-Wideband Optoacoustic Mesoscopy (DW-RSOM) *(previously surfaced — first surfaced 2026-10-15; carried unchanged)*

- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***npj Imaging*** (Nature portfolio); DOI **10.1038/s44303-026-00184-5**; PubMed **42432228** (2026 Jul 10;4(1):51); PMC **13415847**. ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata from prior-run Nature + PubMed snippets.

- **The field's two legs are unchanged.** (1) *Make a vein readable at a named site* — IJV-pulse + breathing modulation (§1.1), sternal/intraosseous reservoir (§1.3), external-jugular reflectance + the Canterbury neck-worn device and CANVOS trial (standing context), and the compliance-pulsation ancestor (§1.5); and (2) *image per-vessel venous sO₂ optically* — human PA at the IJV and radial vein (§2.1–§2.2), intravascular PACT flow+sO₂ (§2.3), per-vessel retinal vis-OCT (§2.4), and microvascular arteriole/venule RSOM (§2.5). The recurring unifier is **artery-as-reference / artery–vein disambiguation**: APM+'s accompanying-artery fluence prior (§2.2), the §1.1 co-registered carotid, the §2.5 515/532 nm ratio rule, and the §1.5 compliance trick are four instances of the same idea at four scales.
- **The shared frontier is the O₂-extraction readout.** §1.1 (cerebral O₂ER), §2.2 (proposed carotid–IJV pair + flow), §2.3 (intravascular flow+sO₂), the K99 award's CMRO₂ goal, and the ULM+PA standing lead all converge on **co-registered per-vessel flow + venous sO₂** as the data a true oxygen-consumption estimate needs. That — not a single better SvO₂ number — is where a genuinely new contribution would most move the field, and the two standing NIH grants make it the most likely near-term source of a rankable new result. The quantitative-PA methods line (GenMC, 3D qPACT virtual-imaging study, Hybrid-Net, SPOI-AE) is the parallel enabling track: **better fluence correction, robust across skin tones, is the common prerequisite** for turning any deep-vein PA signal into an accurate absolute venous sO₂ — the arXiv:2510.03431 virtual-imaging comparison re-seen this run is the clearest recent statement of exactly that.
- **A note on the hepatic thread.** The §2.1 IJV anchor's authors named **hepatic veins** among their explicit extension targets. The two new-to-file mouse hepatic PA papers this run (QIMS confounds study; Southern-Medical fibrosis PAM) show the hepatic-PA sO₂ community is active — but both are tissue/microvascular-level in mice, not non-invasive named-vein venous oximetry. They are worth watching precisely because a *venous-specific* hepatic-PA study would be the kind of extension that would re-populate the ranked list.
