### 2.2 — Arterial Blood-Mediated Deep-Tissue Photoacoustic Oximetry (APM+): In-Vivo Human Radial-Vein sO₂ *(previously surfaced)*

### 2.4 — Adaptive Spectroscopic Visible-Light OCT for Clinical Retinal Oximetry (ADS-vis-OCT) *(previously surfaced)*

**State of the field, restated.** The corpus continues to cluster around one structural problem — **compartment disambiguation**, separating the venous signal from arterial and tissue background — attacked at four scales that remain the live frontier: (1) **single-vessel resolution** (vis-OCT §2.4, PACT §2.3, TD-HSI, fundus-camera and hyperspectral retinal methods) resolves the vein directly; (2) **temporal isolation** (respiration-modulated NIRS §1.2, the IJV-pulse device §1.1, the 2017 compliance-modulation ancestor §1.5, the DNN+Monte-Carlo SijvO₂ tracker) separates the venous pulsation in time; (3) **an anatomical venous reservoir** (sternal marrow §1.3; portal-venous/hepatic sites) reads a naturally venous-weighted source; and (4) **spatial-heterogeneity mapping** (SFDI VOT §2.5) shows tissue-scale micro- vs macrovascular ROIs desaturate differently.
