### 1.1 — Non-invasive, Continuous Venous Oxygen Saturation and Oxygen Extraction Estimation from the Internal Jugular Vein *(previously surfaced — the flagship new-generation true-venous device)*
- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived (bioengineering group associated with the jugular-optical-sensing line).
- **Year / Venue:** 2025 · **IEEE EMBC 2025** proceedings. IEEE Xplore doc 11254941; PubMed 41335695; DOI 10.1109/EMBC58623.2025.11254941. ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata cross-checked on ≥2 snippets.

- **Relevance:** **8.5/10** — directly targets non-invasive, continuous **true venous** sO₂ at the IJV plus cerebral O₂ extraction.
- **Novelty:** Flexible/conformal neck sensor array reads the **IJV pulse + breathing-induced modulation** (peripheral veins being non-pulsatile) to estimate SvO₂ ≈ **65.7–80.9 %** (SD 1.67 % for IJV-pulse, 4.26 % for breathing modulation) and a per-subject **cerebral O₂-extraction ratio (O₂ER)** in 6 healthy adults; co-registers carotid artery and IJV at one neck site, working upright and supine. ### 1.2 — Near-Infrared Spectroscopy for Estimating Mixed Venous Oxygen Saturation and Cardiac Output During Off-Pump Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting: A Prospective Observational Study *(previously surfaced — first logged 2026-09-08)*
- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived — not page-confirmed (PubMed egress-blocked).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · venue **not page-confirmed** (⚠ search-derived; anesthesia/monitoring journal). PubMed 42606675. (Snippet dates publication to August 2026.)
- **Link / DOI:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42606675/ ⚠ egress-blocked; DOI not captured.
- **Relevance:** **7.5/10** — non-invasive SvO₂ surrogate via **jugular + cerebral NIRS**, validated head-to-head against **pulmonary-artery-catheter SvO₂** in adults.
- **Novelty:** Tests two non-invasive NIRS surrogates in 25 OPCAB patients — (a) **rSjvO₂** over the left IJV (bias 2.0 %, wide LoA −14.9/+18.9 %) and (b) **baseline-calibrated cerebral NIRS (cal-rScO₂)** on the forehead (bias 0.8 %, LoA −7.8/+9.4 %, good SvO₂ < 65 % discrimination and trending). **NIRS-derived cardiac output failed** (≈33 % error).

Clean SvO₂ < 65 % discrimination is the clinically actionable low-SvO₂-alarm endpoint.

### 1.3 — Monitoring Changes in Mixed Venous Oxygen Saturation Using Photoplethysmography on the Sternum — A Proof of Concept Study *(previously surfaced — first logged 2026-09-08)*
- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived — not page-confirmed. Likely the Scandinavian intraosseous-sternal-PPG group behind the earlier porcine sternal-oximetry work (*J. Clin. Monit. Comput.* 10.1007/s10877-023-00980-z).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing*** (Springer); DOI **10.1007/s11517-026-03648-3**. PubMed 42527850.
- **Link / DOI:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11517-026-03648-3 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42527850/ ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata via index snippet.
- **Relevance:** **8/10** — non-invasive, continuous **true mixed-venous SvO₂** with a pulmonary-artery-catheter reference; a genuinely new anatomical site (sternum) for venous oximetry.
- **Novelty:** Exploits **sternal intramedullary (intraosseous) blood**, whose saturation tracks central/mixed-venous saturation, read **transcutaneously with a PPG probe** on the sternum. - **Integration insights:** A distinct physical route to non-invasive venous sO₂: the **bone marrow acts as a naturally venous-weighted optical reservoir**, sidestepping artery–vein disambiguation. Trend-grade (~87 %) performance sets a realistic accuracy bar; a natural fusion target for the pulse-modulation math of §1.5.

### 1.5 — Proof-of-Concept Non-Invasive Estimation of Peripheral Venous Oxygen Saturation (arterio-venous compliance pulse-modulation) *(previously surfaced — foundational method ancestor)*
- **Year / Venue:** 2017 · *BioMedical Engineering OnLine* 16:60; DOI 10.1186/s12938-017-0351-x; PMC5437414.
- **Link:** https://biomedical-engineering-online.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12938-017-0351-x · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5437414/
- **Relevance:** **7/10** — the original non-invasive peripheral-venous (SpvO₂) optical concept.
- **Novelty:** Uses the **compliance difference between arteries and veins** to induce artificial respiration-like modulation that makes venous blood pulsatile, then reads the induced PPG to build a calibration model for regional venous sO₂; validated in 8 healthy volunteers with arm-lift/occlusion maneuvers against blood-gas references. Estimated SpvO₂ ran ≈3.1 ± 4.2 % below arterial saturation. - **Integration insights:** The method ancestor of the §1.3 sternal-PPG and §1.1 IJV-pulse devices — every "make the vein pulsatile, then read it like a pulse oximeter" approach traces here. Its modest accuracy is exactly what the deep-diffuse-optics pulsation-decomposition ideas in Topic 2 aim to improve.

### 2.4 — Adaptive Spectroscopic Visible-Light OCT for Clinical Retinal Oximetry (ADS-vis-OCT) *(previously surfaced)*
- **Authors:** Chen Q., Nolen S.L., Spencer S.A., Yi J. (Johns Hopkins).
- **Year / Venue:** 2023 · *Communications Medicine* (Nature), s43856-023-00288-8; PubMed 37095177; PMC10126115.

### 2.5 — GenMC: Real-Time Generative Monte Carlo Surrogate for Quantitative Photoacoustic Imaging *(previously surfaced — first logged 09-27; PA fluence-correction with in-vivo venous sO₂ and skin-tone-bias correction)*
- **Authors:** Mengjie Shi, Feng He, Tom Vercauteren, Wenfeng Xia (King's College London). ⚠ author list search-derived (arXiv egress-blocked; cross-confirmed on ≥2 snippets).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · preprint **arXiv:2609.28261** (physics.med-ph), submitted Sept 2026. ⚠ arXiv egress-blocked; abstract cross-checked on ≥2 index snippets.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28261 · https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28261
- **Relevance:** **7.5/10** — a genuinely new PA *fluence-quantification method* directly enabling quantitative venous sO₂ at depth; demonstrates in-vivo **venous oxygenation ≈ 74.49–77.93 %** and explicitly corrects a bias that most inflates *venous* estimates. Scored below the four PA/vis-OCT anchors because it is a preprint fluence-correction enabler rather than a dedicated venous-validation study, but above the retinal *application* papers because it advances the measurement modality itself and reports venous values.
- **Novelty:** A **conditional GAN** learns tissue optical **fluence distributions** from anatomy + literature optical properties, with **anatomical priors from co-registered ultrasound**. Trained on Monte-Carlo synthetic data, it yields fluence maps in **< 30 ms/frame (~4 orders faster than conventional MC)**, enabling per-frame fluence compensation in real time. It targets the **melanin problem** — elevated melanin in darker skin causes superficial attenuation/clutter that **systematically overestimates venous and arterial sO₂** — and mitigates it in vivo.
- **Integration insights:** (1) Attacks the **same fluence/spectral-coloring bottleneck as APM+ (§2.2)** but by a learned-simulation route driven by co-registered US anatomy — the US-guided geometry the §2.1 human-IJV paradigm already uses. (2) Its real-time budget is the throughput analogue of the DGFT vis-OCT accelerator. (3) The **skin-tone-bias correction** is the single most program-relevant contribution — equitable venous oximetry across skin tones — addressed here *for the venous compartment specifically*. Caveats: preprint, venous values are in-vivo demonstrations not blood-gas-referenced, and it is a quantification method, not a standalone monitor.

## Cross-topic synthesis

- **Today's signal is corpus-completeness, not a new publication.** The two-day window produced no freshly-*published* in-scope paper (consistent with the GenMC/dcBLL publications on 09-27 and the niche's two-to-three-scan cadence), but surfaced two genuinely **new-to-corpus** venous items that 68 prior scans had missed: the **CANVOS ICU trial** and the **2022 sublingual-vein NIRS** study. Both strengthen Topic 1's clinical/anatomical coverage rather than the imaging frontier.
- **CANVOS closes a loop the device line left open.** The §1.1 IJV-pulse EMBC device, the De Gruyter EJV probe, and the UT-Austin e-tattoo all ended at healthy-volunteer proof-of-concept and explicitly named blood-gas-referenced clinical validation as the next step. CANVOS is exactly that study — a 24-hour ICU reflectance-oximetry neck sensor validated against blood-gas SvO₂/O₂ER. It is the Topic-1 counterpart to the regulatory maturity the Mespere VO100 (§1.4) and Noninvasix LIVOx already represent, and its results (when published) will set the real-world accuracy bar the Topic-2 imaging methods (PA at the IJV, §2.1; APM+ radial vein, §2.2) must beat.
- **The non-invasive-venous *site map* now spans six accessible beds** — IJV (§1.1, §2.1, CANVOS), EJV (De Gruyter), sternum/marrow (§1.3), radial vein (§2.2), retina (§2.4 and the retinal HSI/fundus family), and now the **sublingual vein** (new 2022 lead). Each trades off depth, vessel size, and artery–vein separability differently; the sublingual vein is uniquely superficial and venous-weighted, a natural target for a reflectance or hyperspectral probe.
- **The unifying technical thread is unchanged: fluence/scattering correction and artery–vein disambiguation.** APM+ (arterial-prior fluence calibration), GenMC (learned US-prior fluence + melanin correction), and dcBLL (RTE/diffusion scattering correction) are three independent 2026 attacks on the same spectral-coloring error that most inflates *venous* estimates. The equity dimension — GenMC's and the melanin-corrected-StO₂ work's skin-tone-bias correction — remains the most clinically consequential open problem across both topics.
- **Honest bottom line.** Genuine non-invasive *venous* work is a low-volume niche; today confirms that again. No padding with arterial-only SpO₂ work. The day's contribution is two correctly-sourced new-to-corpus venous items and a confirmation that the ranked fives still stand.
