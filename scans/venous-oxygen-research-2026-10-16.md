- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived — not page-confirmed (PubMed/Springer egress-blocked).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***Journal of Clinical Monitoring and Computing*** (Springer); DOI **10.1007/s10877-026-01477-1**. PubMed 42606675. (Snippet dates publication to August 2026.)
- **Link / DOI:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10877-026-01477-1 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42606675/ ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata via index snippet.

- **Relevance:** **8/10** — non-invasive, continuous **true mixed-venous SvO₂** with a pulmonary-artery-catheter reference; a genuinely new anatomical site (sternum) for venous oximetry.

### 2.2 — Arterial Blood-Mediated Deep-Tissue Photoacoustic Oximetry (APM+): In-Vivo Human Radial-Vein sO₂ *(previously surfaced — now fully published in Advanced Science 2026; resurfaced in today's searches)*
- **Authors:** Karteekeya Sastry, Lihong V. Wang, et al. (Caltech COIL). ⚠ author list cross-checked via Caltech publications-by-year + index snippets.
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***Advanced Science*** (Wiley), DOI **10.1002/advs.76366**; PMC13334587; PubMed 42397001. Accepted **8 June 2026** (confirmed on the hosted full-text snippet this run). ⚠ egress-blocked (Wiley + Caltech-COIL PDF DNS-blocked); abstract and results cross-checked on ≥3 snippets.
- **Link:** https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.76366 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13334587/
- **Relevance:** **8.5/10** — human, in vivo, PA, radial artery–vein pair with *venous* sO₂ reported.
- **Novelty:** Uses the high, known saturation of an accompanying artery as an in-situ spectral reference to locally calibrate wavelength-dependent optical fluence (**spectral coloring**) for the adjacent deep vein. In 8 healthy adult volunteers imaging the radial artery–vein pair, APM+ gave venous sO₂ **median 72.3 %, IQR 8.9 %** — tightly in the physiological 60–80 % range — whereas linear unmixing (LUM) gave median 75.2 % but **IQR 34.4 %** (wildly scattered); in ex-vivo-tissue phantoms APM+ median error **2.9 %** vs LUM **9.8 %**. Authors explicitly propose extending to the **carotid–IJV pair in the neck plus flow measurement to estimate oxygen consumption** — the same O₂ER endpoint as §1.1.
- **Integration insights:** The artery-as-reference trick is the PA analogue of the arterio-venous compliance idea (§1.5) and the co-registered artery of §1.1; "most major arteries have an accompanying vein" is the generalization argument that makes this broadly applicable to deep veins. Now peer-reviewed (Advanced Science), it is the strongest published evidence that an in-situ arterial reference tames the fluence problem for venous PA oximetry.

### 2.5 — Recording Skin Oxygenation by Dual-Wavelength Ultra-Wideband Optoacoustic Mesoscopy *(previously surfaced — first surfaced 2026-10-15; the most recent in-scope publication)*
- **Authors:** ⚠ search-derived — Berezhnoi, Ntziachristos, and colleagues (the TUM / Helmholtz Munich raster-scan optoacoustic mesoscopy group).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***npj Imaging*** (Nature portfolio); DOI **10.1038/s44303-026-00184-5**; PubMed **42432228**; PMC **13415847**. ⚠ egress-blocked; metadata cross-checked on ≥3 snippets (nature.com + PubMed + PMC).
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s44303-026-00184-5 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42432228/ · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13415847/
- **Relevance:** **7/10** — human, in-vivo, non-invasive optoacoustic imaging that **explicitly resolves venule-compartment oxygenation**. *Flagged:* this is **microvascular / skin venule-level sO₂**, not a named-vein SvO₂/ScvO₂ — closer to a vessel-resolved StO₂ than to true venous saturation.
- **Novelty:** First dual-wavelength (**515 nm + 532 nm**) ultra-wideband raster-scan optoacoustic mesoscopy (RSOM) — prior RSOM was single-wavelength, so oxygenation mapping was not possible. In 6 healthy volunteers during **post-occlusive reactive hyperemia** (forearm) and a **vascular occlusion test** (nailfold), the system maps microvascular sO₂, **thresholding the 515/532 nm absorption ratio at 0.9 to label vessels below as venules and above as arterioles**. Imaging-based mean sO₂ **80 ± 10 %** vs **87.5 ± 2.5 %** by blood-gas (difference n.s., p = 0.097); saturation rose to **88 ± 7 %** after cuff release. Prototype, small cohort.
- **Integration insights:** The only corpus entry that does **arteriole-vs-venule spectral separation at the microvascular scale** non-invasively — the fine-grained complement to the deep-vein PA anchors (§2.1–§2.3) and the per-vessel retinal vis-OCT (§2.4). The 515/532 nm ratio discrimination is a concrete, transferable rule for the artery–vein disambiguation that §1.1 and §2.2 call their key unsolved problem. Its occlusion/hyperemia protocol is the same physiological maneuver used to make veins readable in §1.5. Because it reports a *compartment-averaged microvascular* value (not an intravascular named-vein sO₂), it sits below the four true-/deep-venous anchors.
