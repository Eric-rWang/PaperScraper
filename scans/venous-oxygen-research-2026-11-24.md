- **Identifier / Year / Venue:** **PubMed 39093817**; **DOI 10.1213/ANE.0000000000007083**; ***Anesthesia & Analgesia*** 2024 Oct;139(4):781–788 (online 2 Aug 2024). Prospective observational validation study.
- **Link / DOI:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39093817/ · https://doi.org/10.1213/ANE.0000000000007083
- **Relevance:** **5.5/10** — a **true mixed-venous SvO₂** non-invasive measurement validated against the **CO-oximetry reference** in children, no pulmonary-artery catheter. ⚠ Scored below the optical ranked five because it is **non-optical** (a breath-by-breath CO₂ / capnodynamic method, not an optical-imaging technique) and its **method family is already in corpus** (adult trial NCT06632197, validation trial NCT04885699, porcine study 10.1038/s41598-024-77483-7) — this is the peer-reviewed *paper* for that line, newly identified but not a new technique.

Companion to the Karolinska thesis on capnodynamic SvO₂ assessment and the 2022 *J Clin Monit Comput* adult precursor.
- **Integration insights:** The **non-optical comparator benchmark** the optical Topic-1/Topic-2 methods must beat — it sets a concrete accuracy bar (≈92 % concordance vs CO-oximetry, trend-capable, PAC-free) for any optical SvO₂ surrogate. Where §1.1 (IJV e-tattoo), §1.3 (sternal PPG) and §2.1 (human-IJV PA) read the vein *optically*, the capnodynamic route infers mixed-venous saturation from **gas-exchange physics**, sidestepping the artery–vein/fluence disambiguation problem entirely — a fundamentally different, physiology-driven path to the same endpoint. It anchors the active-trial cluster already in corpus (adult NCT06632197, validation NCT04885699) as the published evidence behind them. **Watch for an adult peer-reviewed validation and head-to-head agreement vs optical SvO₂ surrogates — the capnodynamic number is the pragmatic bedside standard the optical field is implicitly competing with.**

### 1.6 — Multi-Optical Sensing for Estimating SvO₂ and Hematocrit Trends in Extracorporeal Circulation Tubing *(previously surfaced — first logged 2026-11-21; re-confirmed this run)*
- **Authors:** Yin et al. ⚠ full author line search-derived — not page-confirmed (egress-blocked).
- **Year / Venue:** 2026 · ***Optics Express*** **34**(23):44351–44364; DOI **10.1364/OE.615247**; PubMed **43040720**.
- **Link / DOI:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43040720 · https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.615247 · https://opg.optica.org/oe/abstract.cfm?doi=10.1364/OE.615247
- **Relevance:** **4.5/10** — a **true venous SvO₂** optical measurement with an explicit drift-correction ML method, but ⚠ **in-line extracorporeal (clamp-on CPB/ECMO tubing), not a transcutaneous in-vivo venous monitor.** Included because it is SvO₂-specific and its multi-wavelength + attention-CNN approach is directly transferable to wearable venous oximetry; honestly scored below the in-vivo anchors.

The extracorporeal counterpart of the JTEHM in-line sensor (PubMed 41668774), but adds venous-specific SvO₂ focus, multi-task HCT co-estimation and the drift-reduction result. **Watch for in-vivo transcutaneous translation and external validation; treat as a transferable-technique entry, not a non-invasive in-vivo venous measurement.**

## Cross-topic synthesis

- **A one-day window with one new item — a true-venous *paper*, not a new imaging technique.** Nothing new cleared Topic 2. In Topic 1, the new **§1.NEW capnodynamic pediatric validation** (PubMed 39093817 / Anesthesia & Analgesia 2024) is a genuine, previously-unsurfaced *publication* of a **true mixed-venous SvO₂** non-invasive measurement — in scope — but it is **non-optical** (CO₂/gas-exchange physics), belongs to a **method family already in corpus** (NCT06632197 / NCT04885699 / porcine), and is a **2024 paper surfaced now**, so it is honestly placed as a standing lead and the ranked top-5 of both topics are unchanged and re-verified.
- **Optical vs non-optical SvO₂ — the two camps are converging on the same bedside endpoint.** The capnodynamic line (§1.NEW; ~92 % concordance vs CO-oximetry, PAC-free) and the optical lines (§1.1 IJV e-tattoo, §1.3 sternal PPG, §2.1 human-IJV PA) are independent routes to the same clinical number — continuous non-invasive mixed/central-venous saturation. The capnodynamic concordance figure is the **pragmatic accuracy bar** the optical methods must match; the optical methods' advantage, if realized, is **regional/vessel-specific** readout (jugular → cerebral O₂ extraction) that a whole-body gas-exchange model cannot give.
- **The off-the-shelf / low-cost angle stays a quiet theme.** TOUCAN-1's repurposed commercial pulse oximeters, §2.6's three-LED/one-photodiode StO₂ front-end, and the §1.1 e-tattoo / §1.3 sternal PPG all push to make continuous venous/tissue oximetry **cheap and wearable**. The common bottleneck these cheap devices then face is the same one the expensive ones face — **separating venous from arterial and tissue signal** — which is exactly why the capnodynamic (physiology-based) route is an interesting end-run around the optical disambiguation problem.
- **The fluence/scattering bottleneck remains the optical field's center of gravity.** APM+ (arterial-reference fluence correction, naming UOT/DOT as adjuncts), GenMC and Hybrid-Net (learned fluence), dcBLL (diffusion-corrected Beer-Lambert), and the §2.5 random-matrix multiple-scattering gating all solve one problem: light transport corrupts the raw spectral signal, and uncorrected linear unmixing overestimates venous sO₂ (often non-physically >100 %). Every credible optical venous-oximetry advance carries an explicit fluence/scattering-correction step.
- **Topic 1 remains the true bottleneck.** No new **optical** surface, named-vein, true-SvO₂/ScvO₂ method appeared — the clinically decisive niche (jugular/sternal/peripheral) is still carried by the §1.1–§1.6 corpus plus a thickening cluster of IJV registry trials (NCT06004466, ACTRN12625000866404, NCT06072755) and the non-optical capnodynamic line, all trend-monitors needing larger blood-gas validation / results publication. The honest state of the field is unchanged: **vessel-resolving / microvascular imaging (Topic 2) advances faster than surface absolute-SvO₂ sensing (Topic 1)**, and the §2.1 human-IJV PA measurement remains the one optical result that most directly bridges the two.
- **Convergent endpoint — oxygen extraction.** The §1.1 neck O₂ER device, the §2.2 artery-referenced APM+ (carotid–IJV + flow), the §2.3 PACT flow+sO₂ work, the MOBILE depth-resolved occlusion readout, the ULM+PA co-registration lead, and the fRSOM/DW-RSOM/§2.6 occlusion-challenge platforms all point at the same destination: **co-registered per-vessel flow and oxygenation → regional O₂ extraction / CMRO₂ / VO₂**, non-invasively — the field's real goal, beyond a single SvO₂ number. The capnodynamic method reaches a *whole-body* version of that same consumption endpoint from the gas side.
